Remaining Regular Season

For 2026, completed, partial, and already-scheduled series are all locked. The simulator fills as many additional matchups as possible up to eight opponents per team. If the current schedule makes eight series impossible for everyone, some teams may finish with seven; seeding then uses winning percentage. Use Players to set who is active for each series.
