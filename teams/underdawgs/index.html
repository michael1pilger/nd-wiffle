Franchise History

Underdawgs

Founded by Connor Dove, the Underdawgs were the first expansion team to be taken over by Freshmen. Connor Dove and Danny Donegan remained as the 1-2 punch for the Underdawgs for 3 years and made them perennial contenders. The Underdawgs are also the only expansion team to ever win a World Series after a 2025 overhaul that gave them a deep roster that took them all the way.
