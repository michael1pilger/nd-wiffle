Franchise History

Beavers / Silverbacks

Founded by other league founder Matthew Doty, the Silverbacks, originally named Beavers, remain the best franchise in the history of the league, never having missed a World Series. After winning the World Series in their inaugural season, the Silverbacks have lost 4 consecutive World Series, often in dramatic fashion. However, their dominance is unmatched in the regular season. Doty consistently kept the Silverbacks as the premier pitching powerhouse in the league.
