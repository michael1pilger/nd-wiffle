Franchise History

Ball Busters

The second of three franchises to be founded by a Freshman, Evan Reid founded the Ball Busters. The Ball Busters have found success in large part due to Matt Baldwin, still the only player outside of the class of ‘25 to win an MVP award. The team returns Evan Reid and veteran JT Kaiser this year, hoping to qualify for the World Series for the first time.
