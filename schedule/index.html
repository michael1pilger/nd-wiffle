2026 Schedule

Each team plays eight 3-game series for a 24-game regular season. When a commissioner publishes a completed series, this page automatically changes it from Upcoming to Final and displays all three scores.

With 10 teams, each team skips one opponent and plays the other eight teams once. Series are normally played as one complete three-game set. Rare weather delays do not change the normal workflow: commissioners publish the series once the three games are complete.
