Power rating methodology: 55% current win percentage, 20% strength of schedule based on opponents faced, 12.5% run differential per game, and 12.5% player form. Player form compares each player’s batting + pitching points in the team’s two most recent series with that player’s own 2026 per-series baseline, using only series in which that player appeared. All four inputs are standardized across the league before being combined, so the rating is a relative measure of current 2026 performance rather than a playoff seeding formula.
