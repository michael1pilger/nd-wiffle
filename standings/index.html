Power rating methodology: 60% current win percentage, 10% strength of schedule based on opponents faced, 10% run differential per game, 10% player form, and 10% team record over the last six games. Player form compares each player’s batting + pitching points in the team’s two most recent series with that player’s own 2026 per-series baseline, using only series in which that player appeared. All four inputs are standardized across the league before being combined, so the rating is a relative measure of current 2026 performance rather than a playoff seeding formula.

Playoff probability model: 20,000 simulations of the remaining 24-game regular-season schedule. Early-season results are deliberately regressed toward league parity, with that regression fading as teams play more games. Each simulation also includes team-strength uncertainty, so a 0-6 start matters without being treated as proof that a team is permanently weak. Current record, point differential, future SOS, known future opponents, unscheduled matchups, and games remaining are still included. Seven teams qualify, so probabilities total exactly 700%. A team is shown at 100% only when mathematically clinched and at 0% only when mathematically eliminated.
